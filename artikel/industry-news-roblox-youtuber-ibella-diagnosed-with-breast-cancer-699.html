Roblox YouTuber IBella has revealed she was diagnosed with breast cancer at just 21 years old, opening up about her treatment and what it means for the future of her YouTube career.

In an emotional YouTube video posted on November 24, Isabella revealed she had been privately dealing with her diagnosis since October 1, the first day of Breast Cancer Awareness Month.

Sources indicate that the YouTuber explained that she first discovered a lump in her breast while trying on outfits ahead of the Roblox Developers Conference. Having otherwise felt healthy, she initially suspected it was the result of hormonal changes.

Recent findings suggest that however, after noticing the lump was growing, she underwent a series of medical tests. Her sister even visited a hospital to advocate for her after the earliest available appointments with an oncologist were months away.

Following a biopsy, mammogram, and other tests, doctors confirmed she had breast cancer.

“My whole world just shattered,” she recalled. “I felt like I was watching a movie, but the movie was my life, you know?”.

Despite the diagnosis, Isabella noted her faith in God and the support of her family had helped her maintain a positive outlook.

Having made videos since she was eight years old, Isabella reassured fans that she had no intention of quitting YouTube, but would prioritize her health while undergoing chemotherapy.

She also explained that continuing to upload videos would help her cover her medical expenses.

In a follow-up video on November 26, Isabella revealed she had already completed her first round of chemotherapy and had noticed her tumor shrinking within days of starting treatment.

The YouTuber also revealed she had shaved her head after experiencing hair loss, removing her wig on camera and thanking fans who had made their Roblox avatars bald in solidarity with her.

Isabella thanked viewers for their donations, confirming that the money would go directly toward her medical expenses and treatment.

She also encouraged her followers to take their health seriously, regardless of their age, and get any unusual lumps checked by a doctor.

In a recent development, “I can’t wait till I’m in remission. And I can’t wait till I beat cancer. And I can’t wait till I make that video where I say I beat cancer.”.
