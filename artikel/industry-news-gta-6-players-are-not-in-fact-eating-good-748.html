📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that there are countless ways to spend your time in Grand Theft Auto 6. You can scoop up pricey bundles of cocaine in a seaplane, do deadlifts to get sick gains, and scuba dive to discover marine life and lost pirate treasure. You can steal an elderly woman's mobility scooter and go to a yard sale to find cool vintage T-shirts. But sadly, one thing you cannot do is savor a delicious meal, whether it's on a solo outing or even on a date night with Jason and Lucia. GTA 6 has a vast, deep world with astonishing attention to detail in almost every capacity. But there's one area where the game comes up surprisingly short — the food.

In a recent development, in the early hours of GTA 6, you'll mostly be downing protein bars, apples, and various bagged snacks from the cupboard in Jason's Key Lento bungalow. It makes sense that these quick snacks aren't rendered in vivid detail — they don't have to be. That said, I was hoping that the game's many restaurants would show us something a little more elaborate.

After all, GTA 6 has more than 350 places where you can stop for a snack or a meal, from expensive fine-dining experiences, to farmers market stalls, to hole-in-the-wall fish sandwich shops. But sadly, pretty much every meal in the game kinda plays out like this. Yes, you can see Jason is clearly eating a hamburger, but the overall effect is not very different from tossing a protein bar down your pie hole.

Obviously, GTA 6 is not Cooking Mama. It is not a game that aspires to be "about food" in any way. Then again, there are plenty of non-food games that feature mouthwatering culinary creations that stick in your memory long after you've finished playing. Consider these examples from Final Fantasy 15, Unicorn Overlord, and Yakuza 0:.

Industry observers note that we know that Rockstar is capable of creating extremely detailed food — the Prairie Box combo from in-game fast food spot Chaco's is a precarious Jenga tower of floppy deli meats, an unholy Arby's nightmare given life. And even if food doesn't serve any particular gameplay function — like granting short-term stat buffs or opening up new dialogue — it's still fun to discover all the different creations. It feels like a missed opportunity to further explore GTA 6's riff on southern Florida, because Miami in particular has such a unique food scene. Let me get up close and personal with a pile of ropa vieja in Little Cuba! Let me savor some overpriced sushi on the main drag in Ocean Beach! Let me pick up a massive box of greasy Chinese with Cal for one of our gaming sessions. Heck, even the Rusty Anchor in Key Lento looks like the kind of place that would make a bangin' coconut shrimp plate.

You can make Jason and Lucia overweight in GTA 6, so why not let players enjoy it? A diet that consists primarily of protein bars and apples is no way to live.
