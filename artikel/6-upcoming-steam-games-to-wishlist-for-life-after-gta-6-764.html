📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

With December right around the corner, a very busy year for video games is coming to a close. And while some players might be spending that last month working through Grand Theft Auto 6, there are still a few new PC games coming out before the calendar changes to 2027.

For Wishlister this week, we've rounded up six upcoming Steam games coming out in December that you won’t want to sleep on. It’s a wide-ranging list, spanning cozy games starring mice exploring Sweden to a horror game about sentient instruments. Whether you want a relaxing wind-down after GTA 6 or something more stressful, there are plenty of upcoming games to toss on your wishlist.

Recent findings suggest that following up on 2025’s hit A Game About Digging a Hole, publisher Rokaplay Select is capitalizing on its success with a multiplayer spinoff. A Game About Digging a Hole Together features all the hole-digging satisfaction of the original game, but with support for up to four players to work together at once.

Do you miss your Rock Band glory days? Well, you’ll soon be able to relive them in Stage Tour. RedOctane Games’ debut rhythm game is a Rock Band revival in everything but name. You’ll be able to jam along to a soundtrack of licensed songs using plastic instruments. Olivia Rodrigo, Radiohead, Metallica, and more will appear in the stacked playlist.

Poised to be December’s biggest WTF game, Don’t Fret sure has one head-turning premise. It’s a horror game set inside of a school overrun with musical monsters. It looks like a mod for a Silent Hill game that replaces Pyramid Head with Cassette Head. It’s both creepy and colorful, setting the stage for a very unique horror game.

A throwback to the golden age of mascot-driven platformers, Waltz and Jam looks like a revival of a classic series that doesn’t actually exist. You’ll play as a ghost and its dog as they journey through the afterlife. With levels inspired by the seven deadly sins, Waltz and Jam looks to breathe some delightfully creative ideas into one of gaming’s foundational genres.

Co-op enthusiasts are getting a cozy little treat this December with Hela: Of Mice and Magic. Inspired by the sights of Northern Sweden, you and your friends will play as tiny mice exploring a big world. The pastoral adventure might be the perfect destressor after a few weeks of crime sprees.

Industry observers note that the survival crafting genre is about to get much smaller. Trollheim: Gnomes is a co-op game that lets up to eight players control gnomes looking for shelter in a vast forest. Inspired by Nordic folklore, the early access game looks to capture some of the same magic that has made Valheim a smash hit.
