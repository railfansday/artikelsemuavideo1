📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The brainwashing Easter egg in Grand Theft Auto 6 is a long side mission in which you get the chance to investigate long-forgotten bunkers where a certain president spent some time (and listened to rumba) and where brainwashing experiments had taken place. Like the Vice City portal Easter egg, you'll need to go through a series of steps to finish this mission, but there's no other option if you want to learn the truth about what the government hid from you.

To help you uncover the mystery regarding President Russo, here's how to start the 'Kill The President' Easter egg in GTA 6 and how to complete it.

Industry observers note that to start the "Kill the President" quest chain, you must talk to José Bermúdez, a man sitting in front of the Joe Russo monument in Downtown, Vice City. Be sure to visit him during the day! Choose to ask "What really happened?" after he starts talking. José will tell you to visit his apartment in a salmon-colored building with Cuban flags on Little Cuba NW 4th avenue if you want to know the truth.

Go to his place to find the blueprint of a bunker on the wall, some recordings, and a picture showing President Russo on Sharktooth Island — your next destination. Before you go, be sure to listen to the recording and pay attention to what President Russo indicates, as he'll tell you the code for the next area.

Sources indicate that travel northeast on the map to find Sharktooth Island, on the southern side of Little Hardin Island in Leonard County. Once you reach the island, head northwest to find the bunker entrance and a keypad lock that requires a six-digit number.

According to recent reports, unfortunately, you won't find the code on the island. The information you need to figure out the code was shared by President Russo himself in the recording you found in José's apartment. Now, if you don't want to return to the apartment just to listen to the recording again, don't worry. The code to open the bunker is 010317.

In a recent development, as you enter the bunk, turn right to find an electrical box. Pull the lever up to turn the lights on, then head in the opposite direction. After passing through a couple of small rooms, you will get to a larger room where you'll find some beds and a presidential desk beside a large safe.

Recent findings suggest that sit in the chair behind the desk and look to your right for the "Search drawer" option to appear. Next, pick up the phone. Inside the drawer, you will find a file with three different number sequences listed below the word "Protocol." Keep these in mind.

The next step is to return to one of the first rooms you passed through and listen to the recordings, in which someone mentions the number "5297" and "protocol two." These are the clues you need to open the safe.

In a recent development, if you want a quick answer to how to open the safe in the presidential bunker, you need to move the last number to the front to get the safe code, which is 2975. Inside, you will find Russo's 1922 pistol and a photo. It appears to show part of the map with arrows pointing to a specific location. If you flip it, you can read the word "OBSESS" on the back of the photo. Now, it's time to locate the place in the photo.

Recent findings suggest that the photo you found inside the safe in the presidential bunker reveals the location of another bunker in Grassrivers, Mariana County. You can reach it by heading southwest from the Richardson Hunting Supplies shop, but you can't enter through the main entrance. Instead, you will need to open the hatch, which you find to the northeast.

Sources indicate that as expected of a secret bunker, the hatch is locked, and you will need to enter a six-digit number to open it. Luckily, you've opened the safe in the presidential bunker and seen the word behind the photo. Don't you think some letters look like numbers? If you make the numbers out, you'll get 085355.

Sources indicate that once you're inside the bunker, there isn't much to do besides make your way through the rooms on the left side to reach the electrical box and turn on the lights. Next, return to the starting portion of the area and follow the path through the rooms on the right side until you reach a half-open metal door leading to a control room.

Press the two red buttons on the main panel. The lights on the garage will turn on, and the door to your right will open. You can now enter the garage, where you will find a tank, the Cold War 556 unique assault rifle, some ammo and grenades. From this point on, the main entrance will remain open, so you can use it if you return here whenever you'd like.

Industry observers note that enjoy the rewards, but this quest is far from over. Return to the main area of the bunker and look for the first room on the right side, where there's a chair and a TV that's now turned on.

Sit on the chair, and you will be transported to the past and the game's perspective changes to a first-person view. While in this dimension, you will be trapped in a loop that only allows you to move forward through the corridor. To return to a previous room, you will need to circle all the way back.

The first step is to leave the room and follow the main corridor until you loop back to where you started. Enter the room where you woke up and answer the phone. The voice on the other end will mention that patients have become obsessed with time and that maybe they should get rid of the clock.

Following this clue will lead to the reception area, the only place where you'll find a clock. On the right side of the room, there's a locked door that requires a six-digit number to open. Ignore it for now, because you don't have enough information to discover the code yet. Interact with the clock to time travel to the future, where you'll find a run-down version of the institute. Take another walk around until you reach the corridor before the reception area. A telephone will ring. Jump through the broken window to enter the room and answer the phone. Pay attention to what the scientist says about the three candidates.

Industry observers note that take another lap until you reach the corridor with the candidates' room, where there are multiple beds. Use your focus to reveal the traces showing where three candidates have gone. Now, if you remember what you heard in the last phone call, the scientist mentioned three people in a specific order. To identify them, you must remember what each person did, so you can trace them back to their respective beds. Once you've figured that out, go back to the past using the clock to see the numbers on the wall. Put the password together by using the numbers in the correct order.

If you don't want to spend time playing detective and analyzing the paths in the candidates' room, here's the code: 090607.

With the password in hand, return to the past and open the room on the right side of the reception area. Inside, you will find a control room with a red telephone and a computer. To access the computer, you will need to form a password using the options displayed in the bottom-right corner of the screen. Although it is possible to find the answer through trial and error, there's one way to discover the password: answer the call.

According to recent reports, after you pick up the telephone, the scientist will once again share information about the program and the candidates, including clues you need to figure out the computer password. He mentions something about the target, the date, and the fact that the candidates are losing their sense of time.

Although there are no meditation spots in this dimension for Lucia or Jason to look at within themselves, they can metaphorically achieve this by looking at their picture hung in the lab. Press L2 on PS5 or LT on Xbox Series X, then hold R2 or RT until the area around the screen turns red. Then, look down to see that a little box open, revealing a Duke 1922 pistol. Take it and your training will be completed.

Industry observers note that return to the past by interacting with the clock, head to the computer that you used earlier. This time, select the "Break the Cycle" option. It will open the exterior containment door, also known as the red door, in the reception area. President Russo had been standing in front of it since you started walking around these corridors. However, this time he won't be there. Open the door and enjoy.

After completing the mission, you will return to reality, unlock the "Brainwashed" award tattoo design and find the Brainwasher 1922 unique pistol beside the TV.
