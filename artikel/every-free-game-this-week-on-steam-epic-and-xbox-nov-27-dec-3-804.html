📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that every week, there are limited-time offers that allow players to enjoy games for free, whether as temporary or permanent additions to their digital libraries. Here's everything that frugal and curious gaming fans can enjoy between Nov. 27 and Dec. 3, so they can make the most of their week without spending a dime.

There is one objective in mind when playing Bread & Fred: reach the top of the mountain. Although it can be played solo, Bread & Fred is a co-op multiplayer game at heart. Players should make sure they tell their friends to visit the Epic Games Store and get Bread & Fred before the offer expires on Dec. 3, so they have a partner to team up with for a challenge.

I don't know if the name gives it away, but Firestone Idle Clicker Online RPG is an idle RPG. It's free-to-play, but if gamers head to the Epic Games Store before Dec. 3, they will be able to claim a special bundle worth $100 for free. It'll grant them dozens of skins, tokens, chests, and more, so they can start their adventure on the right foot.

Both PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 owners can enjoy Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 as the cross-gen edition is making its way to PlayStation Plus on Dec. 1. Whether they played the original or not, they can now play the classic skating game in the best way possible withTony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4. It includes expanded features, improved visuals, and upcoming parks, skaters, and tricks.

Industry observers note that with Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, anime fans can make their own character and join their favorites in epic battles with unforgettable action. It also includes fun online features where they can challenge other players or team up to take down giant beasts in online raids.

Industry observers note that horror fans are in for a treat with Amnesia: The Bunker on this month's roster of PS Plus games. Here, players are tasked with surviving their way through a haunted World War I bunker with only one bullet in the chamber of their revolver. There are supplies scattered around the bunker that players must find to craft whatever items they may need to ensure their survival.

It has been revealed that warhammer 40,000: Mechanicus is available for $3.39 — 90% off — until Dec. 7. Warhammer fans won't want to miss this tactical, turn-based entry in the series. It's a great price, but those who aren't sure whether this game is for them will be happy to know that they can try it for free until Nov. 29 as long as they have a Game Pass subscription.

Another great game for Game Pass players to try this weekend is Little Rocket Lab. While it may look like a cozy game on the surface, with its adorable pixel art style, Little Rocket Lab tasks players with building all sorts of automated factories to improve the town and rebuild an impressive rocket ship.

Before November comes to an end, players should also check whether they've claimed this month's PS Plus games and Humble Bundle.
