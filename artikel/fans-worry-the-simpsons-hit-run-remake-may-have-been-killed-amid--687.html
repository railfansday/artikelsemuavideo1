Simpsons: Hit & Run maker Radical Entertainment reformed earlier this year as Radical Games. Fans speculated it may have been working on a remake of the PS2-era classic following comments made by Simpsons creator Matt Groening at D23. But Radical Games studio head is now saying an unannounced project has been canceled.

Sources indicate that “It seems that not even a fantastic project with an absolutely incredible team is immune from the current state of the industry right now, as what we were working on at New Radical Games (although mind-blowingly awesome) was part of a whole gaggle of cancellations. A gaggle, I say!” Darren Evenson, a developer who started back at Radical Entertainment in the 2000s and was part of the newest incarnation of the studio, wrote on LinkedIn (via VGC).

He continued, “Oh, you silly suits with spreadsheets… will you EVER learn? If you don’t make the game, you can’t make the money… and if you don’t make the money, then you don’t get the power… and… well, there’s more to that quote, but I digress.”.

It’s still not immediately clear what new project Radical Entertainment was working on, though the lineage of the team there and buzz about Simpsons: Hit & Run‘s return more than two decades later has led some fans to believe the studio was developing a remake.
