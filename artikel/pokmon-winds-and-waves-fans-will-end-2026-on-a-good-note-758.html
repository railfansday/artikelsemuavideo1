Nintendo seems intent on letting The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time stay in the Switch 2 spotlight this year, so Pokémon fans still don't know when they'll be able to play Game Freak's next major RPG. Honestly, Pokémon Winds and Waves has been in a full-on information drought since the release of the reveal trailer back in early 2026. Well, there's at least one thing fans have to look forward in the realm of pocket monsters now, and it's happening sooner than anyone expected.

While you won't be able to play Pokémon Winds and Waves in the near future, one thing you can do beginning on Dec. 5 is purchase plushies based on Browt, Pombon and Gecqua. The grass, fire, and water starters have all been faithfully recreated in toy form, so you can make the arduous decision of which monster to pick well ahead of the actual game's release date.

In a recent development, perhaps it would help to know a little about each critter? Browt, for instance, is remarked to use photosynthesis to run around energetically — but The Pokémon Company cautions that the grass starter is kinda clumsy. The Shiba Inu-like Pombon apparently has heat-generating organsyet is one of the more affable monsters in the group. And finally, there's Gecqua, who is the most cunning monster of the three.

Sources indicate that here's a better look at each individual Winds and Waves starter plushie. Each one matches its starter’s appearance in the next-gen Pokémon game's reveal trailer to a T​​​​​​​.

Here's the fine print: As of right now, these plushies are only confirmed for Pokémon Center stores in Japan. Given the excitement around the game, it's probably just a matter of time before The Pokémon Company brings the starter plushies overseas as well.

It has been revealed that until then, and despite the information blackout from Game Freak, Pokémon fans still have lots to chew on. Leakers with access to internal documents from a 2024 information breach have steadily revealed unannounced details about Winds and Waves, including the apparent next professor, latest mechanics, Pokémon types, evolutions, and gym leaders, and map details.

And if that's not enough, the year has seen an explosion of fresh Pokémon fan games that might tide over players until Game Freak or TPC give us more to work with.
