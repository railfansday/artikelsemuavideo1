Grand Theft Auto 6’s multiplayer has managed to start “working” for some PC players, even though there isn’t an official release just yet.

In a recent development, when Rockstar Games confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 would initially only be released on consoles, PC players were left disappointed. Many hoped that, unlike Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA 5, they wouldn’t have to wait a long time for the PC port.

According to recent reports, however, there have been some attempts to get an unofficial version of the game working on PC, since leakers have gotten the game’s source code. Rockstar has shut down a Discord server that had managed to get GTA 6 booting on PC, even though it didn’t progress past the Rockstar logo screen.

Now, though, some PC players have a “working” multiplayer system in GTA 6, including proximity chat.

It was highlighted by Developer TeK, who showed a lobby with two people at once. In a short video, the two met up at the airport, driving cars towards each other and interacting.

Sources indicate that as per the developer, this unofficial port is running at 25–30 FPS. PortWatch, a community tracker for unofficial GTA 6 ports, confirmed that the footage is genuine, but has not yet verified the FPS.

They noted it is on a “private build” and “nobody outside the group” has access to it.

Social media people believe that the unofficial ports are opening modders up to an “unholy legal sh*tstorm” that could affect the entire modding community.

In a recent development, “This ruins gaming idc,” another said. “This s*it is why everything will be digital and behind subscriptions,” another added. “At this rate, Claude is gonna get serious guardrails,” another chimed in.

Take-Two has warned modders who want to release an unofficial port, noting that they’ve been “enhancing our legal activities” around it.

It has been revealed that there have been reports that an official PC version will be released in early 2027, with GTA Online in GTA 6 apparently slated for “next year” as well. Twitch CEO Dan Clancy said he’d had conversations with Rockstar about the multiplayer release.

Take-Two has also stated they will support the current iteration of GTA Online for as long as players want to stick around.
