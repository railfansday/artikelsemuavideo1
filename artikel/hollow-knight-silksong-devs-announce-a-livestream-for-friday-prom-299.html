Sources indicate that hollow Knight: Silksong developer Team Cherry has confirmed it will host a 'Cherry Time Special Presentation' on 27th November, bringing more information to light about the game's first substantial DLC offering, Sea of Sorrows. We know that the free update will be nautically-themed - hence the eponymous 'sea' - and will feature latest bosses, areas and so on, but other than that, information about what's coming is vague.

But it seems that's not all; as well as more information about the mysterious upgrade to the hit Metroidvania game, the studio has also declared "a bunch" of other "exciting announcements." Curious.

The Cherry Time Special Presentation will kick off at 7am PT / 10am ET / 3pm GMT / 4pm CEST. I'll embed the video below, so check back here at the time if you need a bookmark.

Recently, and more than a year after its initial release, physical versions of Hollow Knight: Silksong finally began arriving on players' doorsteps. Whilst these editions contained mostly expected information, there was an unforeseen mention of "free content packs" that would be arriving alongside the Sea of Sorrow DLC. Will we hear more about these as part of this newly introduced showcase? We don't have too long to find out.

In a recent development, it seems likely, though. "The world of Hollow Knight: Silksong will expand with free content packs," the booklet read in the physical editions. "Prepare for even more adventures filled with upcoming friends, new abilities, and new ferocious foes!" Team Cherry has repeatedly confirmed plans for post-launch Silksong support, but other than this upcoming DLC, we don't really know what that entails.

Additionally, Sea of Sorrow's original release window was a vague 2026 - will we see it shadowdrop as part of this stream? Stranger things have happened, especially in the world of Hollow Knight….
